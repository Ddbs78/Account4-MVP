# Account4: The Corporate Accounting Interface for Households
## The Problem: The "Budgeting" Failure Mode
The contemporary landscape of personal finance software is characterized by an overreliance on frictionless automation and superficial interfaces. Budgeting apps rely on constraint and shame, which causes high abandonment. Furthermore, 42% of partnered adults keep financial secrets, and the majority of couples fight over one-sided financial decisions because they lack a structured forum to discuss money.
## The MVP Solution
Account4 is a web-based concept MVP that completely abandons the word "budget." Instead, it treats personal finance through the lens of corporate accounting. Designed for dual-income couples, it positions partners as Co-CEOs of their household.
Instead of passive tracking, Account4 enforces a "Monthly Close"—a scheduled ritual with a specific agenda.
## Core Product Mechanics
•	Deterministic Market Engine: The platform generates a fictional "Share Price" for the household. Unlike gamification built on random volatility, this price is a strict, auditable formula based on Net Margin (weight: 40), Equity Growth (35), Liquidity Cover (15), and Filing Streak (10).
•	Just-In-Time (JIT) Education: Research shows front-loaded financial courses explain only 0.1% of variance in behavior. Account4 uses an inline ⓘ affordance to deliver accounting education exactly at the moment of a decision (e.g., explaining why a gym membership is an operating expense, but an AWS certification is R&D).
•	The 10-K Variance Prompt: The system automatically flags anomalies (e.g., "R&D spending spiked 45%") and forces the Co-CEOs to write a brief justification. The audience for this justification is not a fictional shareholder; it is their partner.
## The Tech Stack & Execution
•	Architecture & Business Logic: Conceptualized and defined by David Belikoff.
•	Execution: HTML, CSS, and Chart.js UI prototyping generated via Claude AI to rapidly validate the user interface and gamification loops without traditional engineering resources.
